Has the Self-Deprecating Bear Already Attained Enlightenment?

Published 2026.03.03

#AboutMe #Enlightenment #Chiikawa #WhenIGetObsessed #Nirvana #TheRoadToMicroEnlightenment #FormIsEmptiness #Nagano #SelfDeprecatingBear

I basically do my work at night (though only up until around when the date changes), so naturally, that's also when my head works best.

But today, unusually, I had things arranged so that work ended in the evening, so first I had to think about what to do with that peak-condition time. After thinking it over, I decided to think about the very subject named in this post's title. And so I walked into some random café.

＊　＊　＊

The Self-Deprecating Bear is one of the representative characters created by the illustrator Nagano, famous for "Chiikawa." Translated literally into English, it becomes "Self-Deprecating Bear"; written in Hangul, it becomes 농담곰.

A bear objectively observing a bear thrashing about because things aren't going well

What makes the Self-Deprecating Bear the Self-Deprecating Bear, as you can tell from the image, isn't so much the inner feelings expressed through text, but rather the marvel of the objective viewpoint itself.

This viewpoint, as long as the premise of "self-directed commentary" holds, can never actually be the viewpoint of a third party other than the bear itself.

Which is to say, we can conclude that inside the bear exists another bear with a bird's-eye viewpoint — an observer bear, or, put plainly, a higher bear, or perhaps a "higher-self bear," if you will (though this bear might also just be a slightly depersonalized bear).

A bear objectively observing a bear currying favor

Whatever you want to call it, there seems to be no mistaking that a metaphysical bear exists, separated from the bear-as-body (the bear as phenomenon).

And this metaphysical bear, no matter what situation or emotion the body-bear falls into, does nothing but watch, the way one watches passing clouds — with no good or evil, no right or wrong, no pleasure or displeasure.

That, one could say, is already the Buddha's concept of sopadhisesa-nirvana (a state where the body still exists, but attachment does not), and also Nagarjuna's "form is emptiness" (that things with form have no true substance, and that the substanceless takes on form exactly as it is).

A bear objectively observing a bear drinking to forget something unpleasant

But if we take the position that true enlightenment lies in stripping away even this objective viewpoint, and falling silent into "I am simply, merely, there" — then perhaps this bear is still only halfway down the road to enlightenment?

＊　＊　＊

For what it's worth, that random café I walked into charged me a 500-yen cover fee. It was an old-fashioned, cash-only place where smoking was allowed. Besides me, there was only one other customer. I figured business must be tough. I hoped they'd keep at it.
