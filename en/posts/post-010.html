A Violent Amount of Chestnut Rice, If You Please.

Published 2026.02.15

#Essay #AboutMe #SleepLog #ChestnutRice

I keep having this dream, over and over, of sleeping on a display table in a crowded bookstore. Really. I wake up on a freshly stacked pile of colorful books, slowly sit myself up, and show not the slightest hint of embarrassment about any of it — so much so that the slightly depersonalized version of me watching from above thinks, ah, I see, this is just how things work in this world, and accepts it. After I leave, the pile of books stays dented in my exact shape, and I pour plaster into the dent and become a schoolteacher.

Honestly, whether I wake up on a futon or on a pile of books, it makes no difference to me. Both are a lie, and both are true. It's ridiculous. For the record, today I woke up on a futon.

On winter mornings, everyone ends up wearing the exact same face. With a fox-like, cat-like expression and a furrowed brow, everyone hunches their shoulders slightly and walks fast, hoping their own volume in the world could somehow be just a little smaller. I find myself wondering why the concrete floor, so black at night, is now dazzlingly bright — no matter how I think about it, I'm clearly overthinking this.

＊　＊　＊

There are certain words you just want to say, for their own sake, you know?

Like, I just want to say "wouldst thou." Wouldst thou kindly confirm. Old-fashioned phrasing actually ends up sounding unique, so maybe it's perfect camouflage for someone who's too serious for their own good.

Or like, I just want to say "chestnut rice." I wish every single acknowledgment in conversation could just be "chestnut rice." If I ever become a politician, that's the manifesto I'll run on. Tax cuts and all that — sorry, not my department. My slogan: <Toward a chewier tomorrow than yesterday.>

＊　＊　＊

"Hey, are you even listening? You've stopped caring, haven't you?"

"Chestnut rice."

＊　＊　＊

Or like, I just want to say "violently." She's violently kind. Violently beautiful. Violently cute. Violently...

You should say the things you want to say, as much as you can. As plainly and clearly as possible. Violently, you might say. Which is why what I really want to shout out loud is: wouldst thou kindly bring me a violent amount of chestnut rice. I don't mind if it snows, I don't mind if the Wi-Fi's flying around out there — but once the pollen starts flying, my nose gets stuffed up and talking gets hard, so I'd like to get this said before then.
